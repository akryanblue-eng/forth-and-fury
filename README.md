# Fourth & Fury 🏈💥

> A hard-hitting arcade football game with counter-cheese defense, explosive physics, online rivalries, and a chaotic franchise mode.

**Fourth & Fury** takes the raw energy of classic arcade gridiron blitzers and rebuilds it for a modern audience: defense that can actually adapt to spam, physics-driven chaos that feels earned instead of random, real online competition, and a franchise sandbox that goes gloriously off the rails.

*"Fourth" as in fourth down. When the game is on the line and it's all fury.*

---

## The Pitch

Arcade football has a nostalgia problem and a fairness problem. The games people remember fondly — 7-on-7, no penalties, on-fire streaks, absurd hits — were also games you could win by finding one unstoppable play and running it forever. **Fourth & Fury** keeps the speed, the exaggeration, and the joy, but designs *around* the exploits that made those games shallow at a competitive level.

The core loop is simple to pick up and deep to master: read the defense, pick your poison, and survive the chaos.

---

## Design Pillars

The whole game is built on five pillars. Every feature has to serve at least one of them, or it doesn't ship. See [`docs/design-pillars.md`](docs/design-pillars.md) for the full breakdown.

### 1. Counter-Play Defense
The defense is a real player, not a speed bump. Defensive audibles, dive counters, pileup escapes, press coverage, and adaptive AI that punishes repetition. If the offense keeps calling the same money play, the defense gets the tools to shut it down.

### 2. Controlled Chaos
Fumbles, drops, and big hits are driven by **systems**, not dice. Fatigue, impact angle, ball security, and player ratings decide outcomes — not the Random Goblin of Doom. Chaos you can read is chaos you can play around.

### 3. Online Rivalries
Ranked matchmaking, private leagues, couch co-op, persistent stat tracking, and instant rematches. The people you lose to should become the people you *need* to beat.

### 4. Fury Franchise
A chaotic long-haul sandbox: draft ridiculous athletes, grow rivalries, upgrade stadiums, and survive injuries, suspensions, weather, and increasingly unhinged owner demands.

### 5. Fantasy Mayhem
Custom players, mutant stat builds, absurd uniforms, destructible stadium props, and environmental hazards. The ceiling for silliness is the sky, but it's built on the same honest systems as everything else.
